About Our University

Founded in 2026 by a group of students who thought actual lectures were too quiet, OnionRing University is the world's first fully operational 2D metaverse campus. We have a library, a quad, and absolutely zero fire alarms.

Our Mission

To prove that higher education can be both intellectually stimulating and compatible with your browser's minimum system requirements. We also accept Bitcoin, but only the funny kind.

Our Vision

A world where every student can walk across campus without stepping on actual grass. We are committed to a 100% virtual, 0% pollen environment.
